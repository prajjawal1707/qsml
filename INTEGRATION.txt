DESIGN NOTES

- The generated QUBO omits the constant P * rhs**2 from the squared penalty.
  The independent validator accounts for this constant offset.
- Equality constraints are checked over every assignment of the original
  binary variables.
- At-most constraints are checked over every original assignment and every
  slack-bit assignment. The validator also verifies the minimum slack energy
  for each original assignment.
- Exhaustive validation is deliberately restricted to small test problems.
- Existing solver algorithms are unchanged.
